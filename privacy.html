Chicken Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Chicken Keyboard ("the app") is a personal Android keyboard developed for private use by its owner. This policy explains what data the app handles and where that data goes. The short version: the app has no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads, and it never sells or shares personal data.

Typing and keyboard data

Everything you type is processed on your device by the keyboard itself (spell composition, suggestions, clipboard suggestions). The app does not log keystrokes and does not send your typing anywhere except as described below when you explicitly use a translation feature.

Translation features

When you tap a translate action, the text you chose to translate is sent to the destination you selected: (a) a web AI service (such as Gemini, ChatGPT or Muse) inside a web view where you are signed in with your own account — that service's own privacy policy applies; or (b) an AI provider API using an API key you entered yourself, stored only on your device. Offline translation uses phrase packs stored on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

Screen translation

The screen translation feature uses an Android accessibility service. It reads the text currently shown in the chat app only at the moment you tap its button, shows you the translation inside the keyboard, and does not store or transmit the captured text anywhere other than the translation destination you selected (see above).

Google Drive backup and sync (optional)

If you sign in with Google inside the app, the app can back up your keyboard settings and your custom theme packs to a folder named "Chicken Keyboard" in your own Google Drive, using the drive.file permission (the app can only see files it created). This data is stored under your Google account, under your control; you can delete the folder at any time. The app's developer receives none of it. Web-service sign-in cookies and AI API keys are never included in the backup.

Data stored on the device

Settings, theme packs, your personal phrase pack and translation history you choose to keep are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is intended for its owner's personal use.
